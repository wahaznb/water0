Water0 is an open-source hydration tracker that respects your privacy.

- Log water, coffee, tea, juice and more with one tap
- Personalized daily goal from your weight, activity and climate
- Smart recommendations: morning nudges, catch-up prompts, caffeine offsets, hot-weather extras
- Adaptive reminders that adjust to your progress and quiet hours
- History with per-day totals and trends
- 100% offline: your data stays in a local database on your phone. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Source code: https://github.com/wahaznb/water0 (Apache 2.0)
